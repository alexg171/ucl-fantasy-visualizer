# UEFA Champions League Fantasy — Rules & Scoring (2026/27)

## Squad Selection

- 15-player squad: 2 goalkeepers, 5 defenders, 5 midfielders, 3 forwards
- Starting XI formation must include: 1 goalkeeper, at least 3 defenders, at least 2 midfielders, at least 1 forward
- Budget: €100m to start, rising to €105m after the league phase

## Players Per Club Limit

Limit rises as the competition progresses:

| Stage | Max players from one club |
|---|---|
| League phase | 3 |
| Play-offs / Round of 16 | 4 |
| Quarter-finals | 5 |
| Semi-finals | 6 |

## Transfers

- **Before the competition starts**: unlimited free transfers
- **League phase**: 2 free transfers per matchday
- **Extra transfers**: -4 points each beyond your free transfers
- **Before knockout stages/play-offs**: unlimited free transfers again
- Unused transfers can carry forward, with limits:
  - League phase: max 1 forward transfer carried over
  - Once knockouts begin: none carry over

## Chips

Two chips, each usable once across the season. Cannot be played on Matchdays 1, 9, or 11 (when everyone gets unlimited free transfers anyway).

- **Wildcard** — unlimited transfers; squad changes stay permanently
- **Limitless** — unlimited transfers; squad reverts back after that matchday

## Captain

- Pick a captain from your starting XI every time you save your team — their points are doubled for that matchday
- If your captain gets a red card, you can hand the armband to another starting XI player, as long as they haven't already played that matchday
- You can switch captain between days within a matchday to chase the bigger score

## Bench & Substitutions

- Only players who finish the matchday in your squad score points
- Automatic substitutions kick in only if you haven't made manual changes
- You can manually substitute up to 4 players and change captain between match days within a matchday
- You can't sub off a player who has received a red card

## Price Changes

- Prices are frozen until the Matchday 2 deadline
- After that, prices shift based on player performance and selection

## Deadlines

- First deadline: 8 September 2026 (Matchday 1)
- Turn on notifications in the UEFA Gaming app to avoid missing one

## Scoring Details

**Timing**: Points are usually calculated within 3 hours of the last match of the day; stats (like balls recovered) can be adjusted during this window based on referee/data-provider info, but are locked once finalized for the day.

**Minutes played**: 1 point for any appearance, +1 extra for 60 minutes (excluding injury time). Confirmed when points are calculated at day's end.

**Assists**: Awarded for the final pass, cross, header, or shot leading to a goal — including from corners, free-kicks, and throw-ins.
- Counts if a shot deflects off the post/bar or an opponent and goes in
- Must be an intentional pass — miscontrols/unintentional touches don't count
- Any legal body part counts, as long as intentional
- A small deflection that doesn't significantly change the pass's direction still counts
- Counts if the pass/shot/cross leads to an own goal
- Winning a penalty gets you penalty-winning points instead of an assist (unless it's a handball penalty)
- Solo runs/dribbled goals credit no assist
- Only one player can get assist points per goal

**Clean sheets**: Awarded to players who play 60+ minutes (excl. added time) without their team conceding while they're on the pitch. If already subbed off when a goal is conceded, their clean sheet bonus is unaffected.

**Player of the Match**: Decided by UEFA's technical observer panel after each match; worth 3 points for that matchday.

**Red cards**: Player keeps being penalised for goals conceded after being sent off. A yellow-then-red only counts as a red (no separate yellow penalty).

**Extra time & penalties**: Points keep accruing in extra time, but a player who plays 120 minutes can't double up on appearance or clean sheet points (i.e., no 2x for 2x60 minutes). No points scored during a penalty shoot-out.
